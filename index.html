Moshi Concepts. Building trust into digital payments.

We're a research and product studio working at the intersection of blockchain, applied AI, and stablecoin payments. Our first product is an on-chain escrow platform built on Cardano.

Backed by Draper Dragon

What we're building

An on-chain escrow platform on Cardano. Funds are locked by a smart contract and released when both sides agree the work is done. We never hold them. Built for teams paying contributors across borders, where trust is the expensive part.

Where we're looking

Blockchain infrastructure

The plumbing underneath. Settlement, custody, and the tooling that makes on-chain systems usable by people who would rather not think about chains.

AI escrow and payments

Escrow and payments where one or both parties is an AI agent: agents that can commit funds, prove work was done, and get paid, with a human able to step in when it matters.

Stablecoin payments

Moving value across borders for the cost of a message, and the product and regulatory questions that come with it.

Who we are

Moshi Concepts is founder-led, with roots in the Cardano ecosystem going back to 2021: stake pool operation, open source tooling, and a community of over 30,000 people learning how this technology actually works. We build the things we wished existed.
