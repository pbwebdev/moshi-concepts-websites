01  /  Our first product

Hokan: on-chain escrow for a global economy.

Funds are locked by a smart contract and released when both sides agree the work is done. We never hold them. Built for teams paying contributors across borders, where trust is the expensive part. It ships on Cardano first, with more chains to follow.
