01  /  Our first product

Hokan: escrow infrastructure, built to be embedded.

MVP in active development

Hokan is designed to give platforms the infrastructure to add conditional and milestone-based payments without becoming the custodian of customer funds. Funds move from the payer's wallet into a smart contract, remain locked against agreed conditions, and settle directly to the receiver. Hokan provides the API, SDK and supporting infrastructure. The contract provides the rules.

02  /  The bigger picture

Escrow is the starting point. Programmable settlement is the platform.

Digital commerce increasingly happens between people, businesses, platforms and software agents that may have no existing relationship and no reason to trust one another. We are building infrastructure that lets those parties transact using rules they can verify before value moves.

Hokan begins with non-custodial escrow. The broader platform extends into embedded payments, stablecoin settlement, dispute tooling, payment APIs and infrastructure for human and agent-driven commerce.

The thesis
  • Blockchain is good at moving value.
  • Commerce needs rules for when it moves.
  • We are building that layer.
